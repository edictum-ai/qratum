01 · ManifestoWhy we exist
A short, true thing

Keep every session.
Prove its provenance.

Your agents generate the most valuable data you own — and most of it evaporates the moment the run ends. What survives is unverifiable: a transcript with no proof it wasn't edited. Qratum is the librarian. It captures every session, redacts what's sensitive, and content-addresses the rest by sha256 — so the bytes are exactly the bytes, forever.

Belief 01
The hash is the truth.
Belief 02
Local first, always.
Belief 03
Redact before you keep.
Belief 04
A record you can't verify isn't one.

02 · The markLogomark
Identity / Mark

One mark.
Three readings.

A hash and a dot — #·. Everything in Qratum is content-addressed by sha256, so the hash literally is the product. It shares the family's construction grammar with Edictum and Ductum, but draws what Qratum uniquely does: it addresses.

Hash

Four strokes of a # — the digest. Content-addressed storage made visible: the same bytes always resolve to the same mark.

Dot

The unit of work — one session, sealed and kept. The same dot Edictum and Qratum share; the family's atom, here rendered in yellow.

Digest lockup

Pair the glyph with a truncated sha256: so it reads as a content digest, never a social hashtag.

03 · ConstructionGeometry
Build spec

100-unit grid.
Four strokes, one dot.

01Verticals. Two near-vertical strokes from y=18 to y=78, slanted 6 units left (top→bottom) to give the hash its lean. Stroke 9 units, square caps. 02Bars. Two horizontal strokes at y=40 and y=60, offset left by 2 units on the lower bar. They overhang the verticals by ~14 units each side. 03Dot. One solid disc, radius 7 units, at (84, 72), lower-right. Rendered in Ledger Yellow — the only color in the mark.

QRATUM, in the box.

Paper, ink, and one yellow.
