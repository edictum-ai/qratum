Rules The Flow Must Make Obvious

Onboard is not preserve Onboarding is the UI explaining state and choices. It should not mutate files by default.
Preserve is not prepare Preserve stores raw, unredacted copies. Preparation creates browseable redacted artifacts later.
Background is preserve-only A source hook or OS schedule keeps raw capture fresh. It does not review, export, upload, or prepare all history.
Open is the product qrt open should open Qratum, not qratum.dev, not a JSON dump, and not a static pipeline map.
Delete legacy commands The public surface is init, open, status, doctor, import, sessions, session, and export.
