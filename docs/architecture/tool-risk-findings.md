# Tool Risk Findings

The product term is tool-risk findings, not GAP-style evidence.

Future finding types may include overbroad writes, destructive commands,
secret-adjacent access, write-before-read, network calls without need, and
approval bypass patterns.

This area is inspired by research showing text safety does not automatically
transfer to tool-call safety. Milestone A does not implement tool-risk findings.
