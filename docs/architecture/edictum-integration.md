# Edictum Integration

Qratum observes and explains. Edictum enforces. Ductum executes. Lexicon
teaches.

Qratum must not depend on Edictum. A future bridge may turn Qratum findings
into Edictum policies, such as blocking final handoff unless verification
happened after the final edit.
